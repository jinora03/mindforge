# Creative Brief — Artifact 007: The Charge

**Public title:** _Why does a balloon stick to the wall?_
**Domain:** Signal Ocean · **Artifact type:** Loom · **Duration:** 28s (9:16)

## 1. The one idea

Rubbing a balloon can leave it with extra electrons. A nearby neutral wall is still attracted because the balloon's electric field shifts electrons in the wall slightly farther away, leaving positive charge slightly closer to the balloon. The closer attraction is stronger than the farther repulsion.

## 2. The unforgettable visual

The Observer visibly rubs the balloon with both hands while charge packets accumulate. Then the Observer disappears from the composition: the charged balloon travels toward the wall, the wall polarizes, and the balloon sticks. The viewer should see **physical rubbing → charge transfer → polarization → attraction** as one continuous causal chain.

## 3. Composition

- Keep the wall panel on a fixed side of frame so its role is spatially obvious before the balloon moves.
- During polarization, show the balloon and wall surface together; avoid a closeup that hides their separation.
- Static visuals should feel energetic but not like lightning bolts large enough to imply dangerous voltage.
- Voxel and Toybox may style the balloon/panel differently, but the same semantic anchors must drive the episode.

## 4. Performance / transformation

The Observer exists only for the charging action and uses a dedicated reusable rubbing motion rather than a generic reach. Once charging is complete, the Observer is hidden and the balloon becomes the hero performer through polarization and sticking.

## 5. Text strategy

Keep the charge language accurate but simple. Avoid saying that the neutral wall becomes permanently positive. The key line is that charges **shift** and the opposite charge ends up slightly closer.

## 6. Reusable production assets

This episode establishes `environment.classroomLab`, `prefab.balloon`, `prefab.demoPanel`, `audio.ambience.classroom`, `audio.sfx.balloonRub`, and `audio.sfx.staticCrackle`. Balloon and panel are intended for later air-pressure, elasticity, electrostatics and force demonstrations.

## 7. Success criteria

- The rubbing action visibly creates the charge instead of the balloon charging by itself.
- The Observer disappears once their causal role is complete.
- The viewer can follow charging, polarization and sticking without narration.
- The wall is represented as neutral-but-polarized rather than magically becoming a permanent opposite charge.
- Static effects remain readable in both themes.
- The balloon's final position clearly reads as attached to the panel.
- Full and current-shot exports retain the entire 28-second causal sequence cleanly.
