# Creative Brief — Artifact 015: The Split

**Public title:** _Why does a rainbow appear?_
**Domain:** Signal Ocean · **Artifact type:** Loom · **Duration:** 20s (9:16)

## 1. The one idea

Raindrops refract sunlight and separate different wavelengths because the amount of bending depends slightly on wavelength. Many droplets send separated colors toward the viewer.

## 2. The unforgettable visual

One bright white beam enters a large stylized raindrop, flashes, then six colored beams snap outward in a rapid fan while rain continues around it.

## 3. Composition

- No character; the droplet and spectrum are the protagonists.
- Keep the entire colored fan inside portrait safe framing.
- Rain must visibly move so the outdoor scene never feels frozen.
- The spectrum appears rapidly rather than one color sitting alone for seconds.

## 4. Performance / timing

Question by 0.2s, sunlight begins ~2s, split at ~4.2s, bending explanation ~8.3s, many-droplet connection ~12s, answer at ~15.6s, hard stop at 20s.

## 5. Text strategy

Every caption overlaps a visible transition. No caption gets a dedicated waiting shot.

## 6. Reusable production assets

Introduces `environment.rainMeadow`, `prefab.prismDrop`, `audio.ambience.rain`, and `audio.sfx.prismSplit`. The optical rig is reusable for refraction, prisms, dispersion and wavelength lessons.

## 7. Success criteria

- Entire episode is 20 seconds or shorter.
- Spectrum is unmistakable by ~5.5s.
- Rain remains in motion.
- No idle humanoid appears.
- At least one meaningful visual change occurs every ~1–2 seconds.
