# Creative Brief — Artifact 009: The Conduction Line

**Public title:** _Why does a spoon get hot in soup?_
**Domain:** Crucible · **Artifact type:** Engine · **Duration:** 27s (9:16)

## 1. The one idea

A metal spoon heats because thermal energy transfers from the hotter soup into the cooler spoon and then spreads through the metal. Microscopic collisions and mobile electrons in the metal make that energy transfer efficient. The everyday name for this process is conduction.

## 2. The unforgettable visual

A warm stream begins at the soup surface, enters the spoon bowl, and visibly races along the metal toward the handle. Only after the handle has warmed does a semantic hand enter, touch it, recoil, and leave. The spoon remains the protagonist from first frame to answer.

## 3. Composition

- Keep the soup/spoon rig centered on the reusable kitchen island.
- The stream should begin visibly inside the soup-contact region, not appear from empty air.
- The handle closeup must still retain enough of the spoon shaft to show where the energy came from.
- Warm emissive treatment is a semantic heat cue owned by each theme; content never hardcodes theme palette meaning.

## 4. Performance / transformation

No full-body Observer is used. A reusable external hand appears only for the handle test, touches after conduction has already happened, recoils immediately, then exits. The human element proves the consequence without occupying unrelated shots.

## 5. Text strategy

Use "thermal energy" and "conduction" plainly. Avoid saying that "heat rises through the spoon"; the direction here follows the temperature difference, not gravity. Keep the final definition short enough to read while the glowing spoon remains visible.

## 6. Reusable production assets

This episode establishes `prefab.bowlSpoon` and reuses `environment.kitchenLab`, `audio.ambience.kitchen`, `audio.sfx.heatTransfer`, and generic heat-stream effects. The bowl/spoon rig can support future convection, cooling, dissolving, boiling and material-conductivity episodes.

## 7. Success criteria

- The energy path is visually continuous from soup to handle.
- The viewer does not confuse conduction with convection or "heat rising."
- The spoon's semantic anchors behave identically in Voxel and Toybox.
- The test hand appears only after the handle has visibly warmed and is gone before the answer shot.
- No idle full-body character appears in the kitchen composition.
- The 27-second pacing leaves enough time for source, transfer, result and definition.
