# Creative Brief — Artifact 010: The Restraint

**Public title:** _Why do seatbelts save you in a sudden stop?_
**Domain:** Crucible · **Artifact type:** Mirror · **Duration:** 32s (9:16)

## 1. The one idea

When a moving car stops suddenly, your body tends to keep moving because of inertia. A seatbelt supplies the force that brings your body to a stop with the car and prevents the much harsher stop that would happen against the dashboard, windshield, or road.

## 2. The unforgettable visual

The passenger rig surges forward during a sudden brake, then visibly meets the fixed belt and rebounds while a ghost trajectory continues ahead. The belt is not decoration: it is the thing that changes the passenger's motion.

## 3. Composition

- Keep the seat rig large in frame; the vehicle cabin exists to communicate speed, not to compete with the demonstration.
- Roadside stripes establish forward motion while the passenger surge and brake impact carry the stop beat.
- Use a side/front three-quarter camera so the forward surge and belt contact are readable on a phone.
- No standing Observer appears. The seated passenger inside the reusable rig is the actor because the concept is about a passenger's motion.

## 4. Performance / transformation

Cruise calmly, latch the belt, then make the stop abrupt: passenger forward motion, belt contact, short recoil, settle. The ghost trajectory after the stop shows what inertia would continue to do without turning the scene into a second character performance.

## 5. Text strategy

Caption-led until production narration is imported. Structure: moving together → car stops → body keeps moving → belt supplies the stopping force → safer outcome.

## 6. Reusable production assets

Establishes `environment.vehicleCabin`, `prefab.seatRig`, `audio.ambience.road`, `audio.sfx.beltLatch`, and `audio.sfx.brakeImpact` for future inertia, collisions, reaction-time, and transport episodes.

## 7. Success criteria

- The passenger's forward surge is obvious before the explanation text appears.
- The belt visibly intersects the causal action rather than being a static prop.
- The ghost path clearly communicates continued motion without a belt.
- No idle humanoid is present.
- Voxel and Toybox share the same seat/passenger/belt/latch anchors and timings.
- Full export is exactly 32 seconds and remains deterministic after seeking.
